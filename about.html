About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began with my grandmother, Grandma Betty. Every summer my family would drive up to a small town in Tennessee to visit. The smell of fresh bread always greeted our family at the door. And it wasn't when we arrived. She baked bread everyday, and on the weekends she always made a cake.

Not only was Grandma Betty an amazing baker, but she was also fascinated with the sky and the formations the stars made. Ever since I was a little girl, Grandma Betty would take me outside at night and point at the north star. She'd say "if you can find your north star, everything else comes easy."

Grandma Betty passed away in 2010, but that's when I found my north star and decided to carry on her legacy and her recipes. North Star Bakery was formed and we've been aiming for the stars ever since!

How We Source our Ingredients

At North Star Bakery we want to serve our community. We buy as many ingredients as we can from merchants in the area. We also work with local farmers to source our fruits, cheeses and milk.
